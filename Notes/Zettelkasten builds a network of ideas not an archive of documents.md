## Review Question
Why does Zettelkasten compound understanding faster than a folder-based note system?

> [!abstract] Essence (Answer)
> Zettelkasten forces you to reformulate every idea in your own words and explicitly connect it to what you already know. Folders store documents — Zettelkasten builds a meaning network where each new note strengthens existing ones.
